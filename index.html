🚀 Operations & Performance Management Playbook

This comprehensive study playbook integrates Financial Management and Performance Management. It is divided into 4 core phases. At the end, you'll find a dynamic, randomized quiz designed to be taken multiple times so you can test your mastery of all topics!

Phase 1: Financial & Commercial Acumen

Understanding how operational decisions impact the bottom line is critical for Delivery Leaders.

Key Cost Drivers

  • Direct Expense (COGS): Cost of driving revenue (Agents, TLs, QA, Trainers).
  • OPEX (G&A): Indirect costs (HR, IT, Facilities).
  • Inactive People: Manage benched agents and training overlap. Keep training attrition low.

Revenue Billing Models

  • Per FTE: Fixed rate. Maximize filled seats.
  • Per Worked Hour: Paid for login. Optimize attendance & shrinkage.
  • Per Production Hour: Paid when not in AUX. Minimize AHT & AUX abuse.
  • Per Transaction: Maximize volume handled.
💰 MLOTT (Money Left on The Table)
The cost of missed opportunities! Compare the cost of taking a penalty vs. the cost of overtime to fix it. Always calculate the net ROI.

The Staffing Waterfall Math

How to step down from raw volume to physical headcount needed:

  1. Raw Hours: Total Transactions × AHT.
  2. Base People: Raw Hours ÷ Standard Work Hours per Year.
  3. Attendance (Not-in-Office Shrinkage): Divide Base People by Attendance Rate.
  4. Utilization (In-Office Shrinkage): Divide by Production Utilization Rate.
  5. Occupancy: Divide by Occupancy Rate.

Phase 2: Performance & Quartile Management

Structured performance management drives consistent delivery and agent growth.

The 4 Components of Performance Management

  • Planning: Establishing goals and objectives.
  • Developing & Reviewing: Creating PDPs (Personal Development Plans) and performance reviews.
  • Monitoring: Regularly tracking progress.
  • Recognition & Rewards: Rating and recognizing high performers.

Managing by Quartiles

Quartile Action Items & Strategy
Q1 (Top Performers) Motivate them! Provide minor opportunity coaching and develop them as a prize (career pathing).
Q2 & Q3 (Middle Performers) Focus heavily on development. Assign support tasks to build confidence and skills.
Q4 (Bottom Performers) Work closely on performance. Assign a peer mentor, focus entirely on RCA (Root Cause Analysis), follow up strictly, and ensure accountability.

Phase 3: Coaching Excellence & RCA

Effective coaching changes behavior, builds trust, and ensures process compliance.

The GROW Coaching Model

  • G - Goal: Establish desired outcome.
  • R - Reality: Assess current situation (SMART, tangible).
  • O - Options: Brainstorm alternatives and action items.
  • W - Will: Commit to decisions and follow-up.

The STAR Interview/Feedback Model

  • S - Situation: Set the scene.
  • T - Task: What needed to be achieved?
  • A - Action: What course of action was taken and why?
  • R - Result: The outcome of the actions.

Psychology in Coaching

  • The Endowment Effect: People value what they "own." Give agents a sense of ownership over their action plans. Make them come up with their own solutions!
  • Pre-Coaching Prep: Plan 30 mins before. Actual prep time should not exceed 15 minutes. Rely on Hard Evidence.
  • Handling Pushback: Never just say "NO." Highlight loopholes in their arguments so they reach their own conclusions.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA)

Don't treat the symptom; cure the disease.

  • Symptom: What you see (e.g., NPS dropped). Fixing this provides short-term relief.
  • Root Cause: The hidden issue (e.g., Agent lacked empathy due to stress). Fixing this provides a long-term fix.
  • RCA Tools: 5 Whys, Fishbone Diagram (Ishikawa), Pareto Analysis (80/20 rule), and Deep Dive Analysis (MoM, WoW, DoD).

Phase 4: Operational Metrics & WFM Compliance

The numbers that dictate the health of the floor.

Core WFM Formulas

  • Utilization %: (Productive Time + Available Time) ÷ Paid Hours Onsite.
  • Occupancy %: Productive Time ÷ (Productive Time + Available Time).
  • AHT: Talk Time + Hold Time + ACW (Wrap-up).
  • Absenteeism %: Missing Hours ÷ Scheduled Hours.
  • Attrition %: Heads needing replacement ÷ Total Headcount.

SLA vs Interval Compliance (IC)

  • SLA: % of calls answered within a threshold (e.g., 80% in 20s). Focuses on Customer Experience. Aggregated across all intervals.
  • Interval Compliance (IC): Productive FTEs ÷ Commitment per interval. Focuses on Operational Control. Identifies if staffing is aligned with forecasts per 30-min block.

Reactive vs Proactive Staffing

  • AHOD (All Hands On Deck): Reactive, short-term emergency measure for sudden spikes. Everyone jumps on calls.
  • Caplift: Proactive, structural solution for sustained demand (e.g., raising overtime caps, adding shifts).

Voice of the Customer (VOC)

  • CSAT %: Positive Surveys ÷ Total Surveys.
  • DSAT %: Negative Surveys ÷ Total Surveys.
  • NPS (Net Promoter Score): % Promoters - % Detractors.
